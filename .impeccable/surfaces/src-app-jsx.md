Scope: LUMEN marketing site (single page, all sections). Visitor mode: Persuade.
Audience: Georgian business owners (Tbilisi first, plus abroad) who either want ideas for a site or already want to order a site or ads. Action: get a price in the builder and send a brief; secondary: browse site ideas.
Constraints: bilingual ka/en; no invented proof (stats, testimonials, clients); prices are placeholders the owner must confirm.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The page is a lit stage where one faceted crystal splits light across the studio's name. It refuses the category's gradient-blob hero with glowing purple cards and icon-card grids.
OWN-WORLD: Ink-black ground, bone-white type, warm stone grey. There's no UI accent hue: colour arrives only as spectral dispersion through real 3D glass. Wide Archivo display (Noto Sans Georgian for ka), bone pill buttons, hairline rules, large typographic index rows instead of cards.
STORY: The visitor sees the craft instantly (the site is the proof), browses the idea reel or configures a package with a live estimate, then sends the brief.
FIRST VIEWPORT: A full-bleed canvas. The giant LUMEN wordmark spans the width behind a refracting crystal (centre-right).
