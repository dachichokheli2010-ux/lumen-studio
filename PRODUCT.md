# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack
Vite + React + Three.js via react-three-fiber/drei for real-time 3D, GSAP ScrollTrigger for scroll choreography, Lenis smooth scroll. Developed in VS Code, run with `npm run dev`. (User chose the recommended "React + 3D pro stack", 2026-11-10.)

## Users
Business owners in Georgia (Tbilisi first, plus clients abroad) who want a website or ads. Two situations:
- They want a site but don't know what it should look like, so they come to see ideas.
- They already know what they want, so they come to order a site, ads, or "everything".

## Product Purpose
Marketing site for LUMEN, a studio that designs and sells websites (including 3D/immersive sites) and runs ad campaigns. Success means a visitor either finds a direction they like or configures what they need, then sends an inquiry.

## Positioning
The site itself is the portfolio: it should prove the studio can build premium, 3D, award-level web experiences by being one.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Idea gallery of site styles by industry, with previews.
- Package builder with an instant estimate that becomes an inquiry.
- Services: web design and build, e-commerce, 3D/immersive, ads and growth, SEO, care plans.
- Contact or inquiry form. There's no backend yet; submission needs a form service later.
- Bilingual: Georgian and English with a language switch.
- Open: real prices, contact details and the domain aren't confirmed yet.

## Brand Commitments
Name: LUMEN (placeholder, may change). The user requires the result to look premium and comparable to top agency sites, with real 3D. Built and delivered as a VS Code project, not as a hosted artifact.

## Evidence on Hand
None yet: no real client work, testimonials, stats or prices exist. Any shown are illustrative and must be labeled or replaced before launch.

## Product Principles
1. The site is the proof: craft and 3D quality do the selling.
2. Two paths, inspire and order, are both reachable from the first viewport.
3. Never invent social proof as fact.
4. Georgian is a first-class language, not a translation afterthought.
